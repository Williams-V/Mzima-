Our approach

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. If we add analytics or other tracking technologies in future, we will update this policy and provide appropriate controls.

What the site stores

The contact form may keep a small counter in your browser’s session storage to limit repeated submissions. It is stored only on your device and is removed when the tab is closed.

Third-party content

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to retrieve them, and Google’s own policies apply to that request.

Managing storage

You can clear or block cookies and site storage in your browser settings.
