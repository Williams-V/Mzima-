Simple guides to help you get the most from your project.

Project enquiry brief

What to include

  • What problem are you trying to solve?
  • Who will use the solution?
  • What are your main goals and how will you measure success?
  • Which features are essential, and which are nice to have?
  • Do you have existing systems, websites or data we need to work with?
  • What is your approximate budget and timeline?
  • Who will make decisions and give feedback?
Client preparation checklist

Before we talk

  • Gather examples of websites or apps you like
  • Collect your logo, brand colours and any existing content
  • List the third-party tools you currently use
  • Think about who will manage the product after launch
  • Note any deadlines or events that affect timing
Downloadable documents such as a company profile and service overview will be added here once they are approved.
