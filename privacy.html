Privacy Policy

Please read this carefully.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Mzima is a software development company. This policy explains how we handle information when you use this website.

Information we collect

If you use our contact form, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, optional company name, optional phone number, service of interest, optional budget, project description and preferred contact method. We only ask for what we need to respond to you.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry and to discuss your project. We do not sell your information.

Third parties

Form submissions are delivered through the email or form-handling service configured by Mzima. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google when you visit. Links to GitHub and other sites are governed by those sites’ own policies.

Storage in your browser

To limit repeated submissions, the contact form may store a small counter in your browser’s session storage. It stays on your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us and to keep it only as long as needed for the purpose it was provided.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us through the contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
