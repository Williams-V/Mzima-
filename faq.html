Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how we work.

What services does Mzima provide?

We offer website development, web applications, custom software, AI and intelligent solutions, API and backend development, database solutions, automation, and maintenance and support. See the Services page for details.

How does a project begin?

It starts with a conversation. Send us your idea through the contact form, and we will discuss your goals, users and constraints before agreeing on scope and next steps.

How is project pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity and timeline of what you need. We agree the details with you before work begins.

How long does development take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. A simple website is quicker than a custom application. We agree a timeline and milestones during planning.

Can Mzima maintain an existing website?

Yes, we can provide maintenance and support for existing websites, including bug fixes, updates, performance improvements and security upkeep. Share the details and we will confirm what is possible.

Can clients request changes?

Yes. We build collaboratively, and we expect needs to evolve. How changes are handled (for example, within scope or as an addition) is agreed in advance so there are no surprises.

Who owns the final deliverables?

Ownership and usage terms are agreed in writing before work starts, so both sides are clear.

Does Mzima provide support after launch?

We can provide ongoing maintenance and support according to the arrangement we agree together.

Can Mzima integrate third-party APIs?

Yes. API integration is part of our backend and API development service. Tell us which services you need to connect and we will advise on the best approach.

Still have a question? Get in touch.
