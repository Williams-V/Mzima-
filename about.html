About Mzima

A software company built on practical problem-solving, clear communication and quality engineering.

Who we are

Software engineers who build around your problem

Mzima is a software company created by three engineers who collaborate across the entire development lifecycle.

Our approach to software engineering is practical: understand the problem first, choose the simplest solution that will work well, and build it so it stays easy to maintain. We aim to help with digital challenges such as getting a business online, replacing slow manual processes, connecting systems together and exploring where AI can genuinely help.

Our Mission

Practical, reliable digital solutions

To develop practical, reliable digital solutions that help people and businesses solve problems through technology.

Our Vision

Useful innovation, lasting impact

To grow into a trusted technology company known for useful innovation, quality software and meaningful digital impact.

Our Approach

From problem to maintained solution

We work with you to understand the problem, define what success looks like, then design, develop, test and deploy the solution. After launch we stay available for improvements and support, according to what we agree together.
